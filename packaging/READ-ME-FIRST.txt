OCEAN - English -> Persian dictionary for learners
=================================================

START
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. Documents\Ocean). Keep all files together.
  2. Double-click Ocean.exe.
     Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only needed the first time.

USE
  - Type an English word, idiom or sentence and press Enter.
  - Click the speaker buttons to hear pronunciation.
  - Click "Save" (star) on a word to add it to your review deck; review it
    from the "Review" button on the home screen (Space shows the answer,
    keys 1-4 grade it).
  - Look up from any app: copy a word (Ctrl+C), then press Ctrl+Alt+O while
    Ocean is running.
  - Menu > History > Export to Anki makes a file you can import into Anki.
  - Menu > Pronounce reads any English text aloud with its IPA.

Works offline: the dictionary is on your computer. Without internet, Persian
the dictionary doesn't have comes from an offline translator (lower quality).

YOUR DATA
  History, saved words and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\Ocean, so you can
  replace this folder with a newer version without losing anything.
